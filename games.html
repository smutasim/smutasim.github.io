Wild Isle Zoo

An idle, tap-to-build zoo game in the spirit of the old Tap Zoo. Buy exhibits, collect coins from your visitors, hire staff, and grow the zoo's rating over time. It runs entirely in the browser, and it keeps ticking even while the tab is in the background.
