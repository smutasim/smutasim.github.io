Positions come from JPL's approximate Keplerian elements, good from about 1800 to 2050. Magnitudes, ring geometry, and moon positions use standard low-accuracy methods (Meeus). Planet surfaces are procedural renders for identification, not photographic maps. Full notes are at the bottom of the tool.
