Blocking the Sun with Satellites

January 2026

Can We Block the Sun with Satellites to Stop Climate Change

A look at Elon Musk's idea of using satellite constellations at L1 or in LEO to dim incoming sunlight enough to offset warming. Works through the Earth's energy budget, how large and how heavy such a constellation would actually need to be, and why the numbers make it wildly impractical with current technology, plus a couple of side tangents on space-based solar power and terraforming Venus.

Why We Can Technically Travel Faster than Light (FTL)

No wormholes or warp drives required. A walkthrough of special relativity, time dilation, and length contraction to show why a traveler's own experienced time and distance let them effectively outrun light on a one-way trip, and why acceleration, not the speed of light, is the real limit on how fast we could get somewhere.

Imaging Exoplanets using the Sun (and other methods)

Why directly imaging an exoplanet's surface is so hard, and a tour of the ways around it: segmented mirrors, optical interferometry, and solar gravitational lensing, including the math behind minimum focal distances. Ends on a speculative detour into using a manufactured black hole as a lens instead, and why that turns out to be a worse idea than it sounds.
